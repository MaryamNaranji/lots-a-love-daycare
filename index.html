About Us

A loving place to feel safe, happy, and encouraged

Lots A Love Daycare is a licensed home-based daycare located in Potomac Station, Leesburg, Virginia. We care for children from birth through age 12 in a warm, family-style environment where every child can learn, play, explore, and build confidence.

Our goal is to give parents peace of mind while providing children with dependable routines, age-appropriate learning, creative activities, nutritious food, and plenty of positive attention.
