Welcome to Lots A Love

A happy little place that feels like home

Lots A Love Daycare is a licensed home-based daycare in Potomac Station, Leesburg. We provide dependable, nurturing care in a comfortable setting where children can learn, play, create, and build confidence.

Our days are filled with caring routines, age-appropriate learning, creative activities, outdoor fun, nutritious meals, and plenty of positive attention.

Our Programs

Care for every stage of childhood
